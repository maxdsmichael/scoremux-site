ScoreMux Support

ScoreMux is a sheet-music app for iPhone and iPad: import the PDFs of your parts, and it finds the songs, lets you mark and cut one measure at a time, and keeps your book show-ready.

Get help

Email maxdsmichael@gmail.com — it goes straight to the developer. Please include your device model and what you were doing when the problem occurred; screenshots help a lot.

Beta testers: the fastest route is TestFlight itself — take a screenshot inside ScoreMux, and TestFlight will offer to send it with your notes attached.

Common questions

How do I get my music in?
Open ScoreMux and tap Import, or share a PDF to ScoreMux from Files, Mail, or any app with a share button. Scanned books work — that's the whole point.

Where is my stuff stored?
ScoreMux saves on this device first. When iCloud is available, it also keeps private copies in the iCloud storage for your Apple ID. No ScoreMux account is required. The app continues to work when the device is offline.

Why does ScoreMux ask for the microphone?
Only for the tuner, only while it's open, and nothing is ever recorded. See the privacy policy.

Does it change my original PDFs?
Never. Your imported files are kept as-is; markings, cuts, and re-arrangements live alongside them and can always be turned off or undone.
