## Reuse terms
https://www.mutopiaproject.org/legal.html explains that the Public Domain label means the contributor dedicated their contribution to the public domain, permitting distribution, changes, sales, and performance. None of these three selected editions needs Creative Commons attribution or share-alike compliance. A courtesy credit to Mutopia and the typesetters remains useful provenance.
