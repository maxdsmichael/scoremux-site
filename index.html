ABOUT SCOREMUX

ScoreMux is a sheet-music app for iPad and iPhone. Import the PDFs of your parts and it finds the songs, so you open a song instead of a page number. Write on your music with a finger or Apple Pencil. Cut and re-arrange the show one measure at a time, and the original file is never changed. Line the same music up across more than one book. A tuner and a metronome sit one tap from the music, and everything works offline. Private copies sync through your own iCloud, with no ScoreMux account, no ads, and no tracking.
