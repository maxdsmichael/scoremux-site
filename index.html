ScoreMux

A sheet-music app for iPhone and iPad. Import the PDFs of your parts, and it finds the songs, lets you mark and cut one measure at a time, and keeps your book show-ready. A tuner and metronome ride along on the stand.

Your work saves on your device first. Private copies use your own iCloud. There is no ScoreMux account, and the app does not collect any data.

ScoreMux is in beta. Questions: maxdsmichael@gmail.com
